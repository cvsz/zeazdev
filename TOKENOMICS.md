1. **Staking Rewards**: New ZEA minted
   - Year 1: 5% inflation
   - Year 2: 4% inflation
   - Year 3: 3% inflation
   - Year 4+: 2% inflation
   
2. **Liquidity Mining**: New ZEA minted
   - Decreasing over time
   - Halving every 2 years

#### Deflationary Mechanisms
1. **Transaction Burns**: 0.1% of all transfers burned
2. **Trading Fee Burns**: 10% of trading fees burned
3. **Game Burns**: ZEAZ burned in certain game actions

**Target Equilibrium**: 
- Long-term inflation: ~1-2% annually

- Net neutral to slightly deflationary
